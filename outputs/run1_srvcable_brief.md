## Snapshot
- For Q1 FY27, the company reported revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore in official filings. [S3]
- Q1 FY27 EBITDA was achieved at ₹140 crore, representing an EBITDA margin of 11.2%. [S3]
- Profit after tax for Q1 FY27 reached ₹82 crore. [S3]
- The company's order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026. [S3]
- Promoter holding was reported at 58.2% as of 30 June 2026, with 4.1% of promoter holding pledged. [S8]

## Bull case
- Management maintains a full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent. [S2]
- The new Bharuch plant is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding approximately 30% to cable capacity. [S3]
- Exports grew to contribute 21% of total revenue in Q1 FY27 compared to 16% in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- Data-centre cables represent about 9 percent of domestic revenue, with management projecting this share can double within two years. [S2]

## Bear case
- Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points year-over-year down to 11.2%. [S3]
- Working capital pressure is evident as receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the past year, primarily due to slower payments from two state utility customers. [S2]
- The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore covering FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S7]
