## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables Ltd reported Q1 FY27 revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore, representing an 18.0% year-on-year increase compared to ₹1,058 crore in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- The company reported a Q1 FY27 EBITDA of ₹140 crore, a 2.6% year-on-year increase from ₹136 crore in Q1 FY26. [S3]
- Q1 FY27 EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points to 11.2% from 12.9% in the corresponding period of the previous year. [S3]
- Profit after tax for Q1 FY27 stood at ₹82 crore, marking a 5.9% year-on-year increase compared to ₹77 crore in Q1 FY26. [S3]

## Bull case
- The company's order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, increasing from ₹3,150 crore a year ago. [S3]
- Management has maintained its forward-looking full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent, supported by expected contributions from the Bharuch facility in the second half. [S2]
- Promoters' pledged shares as a percentage of total promoter holding moderated to 4.1% as of June 30, 2026. [S8]

## Bear case
- Receivable days lengthened from 78 to 96 over the past year due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S2]
- The company disclosed a regulatory demand order of ₹46.3 crore raised by the Joint Commissioner, CGST, Mumbai, regarding input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S7]
- Net debt to equity was reported at 0.35 times, with ongoing funding requirements for the remaining portion of the ₹620 crore Bharuch capital expenditure. [S2]
