## Snapshot
- Sarvottam Cables Ltd reported official Q1 FY27 revenue from operations of ₹1,248 crore, representing an 18.0% year-on-year increase. [S3]
- Reported EBITDA for Q1 FY27 stood at ₹140 crore, achieving an EBITDA margin of 11.2% which reflects a 170 basis point contraction year-on-year. [S3]
- Profit after tax for the quarter was reported at ₹82 crore, marking a 5.9% increase compared to the same period in the previous year. [S3]
- The company's order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, rising from ₹3,150 crore a year prior. [S3]

## Bull case
- Management maintains its forward-looking revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent for the full year FY27, supported by contributions from Bharuch starting in the second half. [S2]
- Data-centre cables constitute approximately 9 percent of domestic revenue, with management projecting this segment could double within two years. [S2]
- The Bharuch capital expenditure totals ₹620 crore, with about 70 percent already spent, while maintaining a moderate net debt to equity ratio of 0.35 times. [S2]

## Bear case
- Receivable days lengthened from 78 to 96 over the trailing year due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S2]
- The company received a tax demand order of ₹46.3 crore from the Joint Commissioner, CGST, Mumbai, concerning input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S7]
