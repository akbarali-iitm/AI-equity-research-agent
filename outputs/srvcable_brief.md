## Snapshot
- The company reported Q1 FY27 revenue from operations at ₹1,248 crore. [S3]
- Q1 FY27 EBITDA was ₹140 crore, with an EBITDA margin of 11.2%, down 170 basis points year-over-year. [S3]
- Q1 FY27 profit after tax was reported at ₹82 crore. [S3]
- The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of June 30, 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior. [S3]
- Promoter shareholding was 58.2% as of June 30, 2026, with 4.1% of promoter holdings pledged. [S8]

## Bull case
- Management maintained its full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent. [S2]
- The new Bharuch plant in Gujarat is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding approximately 30% to cable capacity. [S3]
- Exports contributed 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27, increasing from 16% in the year-ago period. [S3]
- Data-centre cables represent about 9 percent of domestic revenue, with management projecting this share to double in two years. [S2]

## Bear case
- Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the trailing year due to slower payments from two large state utility customers. [S2]
- The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore, including interest and penalty, relating to input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22. [S7]
- EBITDA margin contracted by 170 basis points year-over-year to 11.2% in Q1 FY27. [S3]
